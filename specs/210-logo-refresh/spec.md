# Spec 210 — Logo refresh: one drawing in two weights

## Context

Step 0 of the launch plan (spec 209, PR #1259): the mark is the first thing every later step reuses (favicon, social
card, video, showcase site). On 2026-11-14 the maintainer reviewed the logo and kept its idea (outline house, soft
blue face, wink) but found three weaknesses:

- **The face is small** inside the house and the stroke is thin, so below about 24 px the smile and eyes vanish.
- **There are three drawings**, not one: login and splash draw a two-tone mark (outline 8 to 9, face 5 to 9), the
  sidebar and the mobile header a monochrome one, the favicon and the PWA icons another (outline 10 to 12, face 7 to 9,
  a wider face).
- **The wordmark differs everywhere**: « SOWEL » spaced capitals in the app (login, splash, sidebar), « Sowel » in Inter
  on the docs landing.

Directions were drawn and compared in context (tab 16 px, 32 px, app icon, light and dark headers, login size) on a
design canvas; a solid blue house was tried and rejected (« ne rend pas bien visuellement »). The maintainer chose
« affinage 1 »: the existing outline house with a refined face, then asked for the face to sit slightly higher, to be
a little smaller and closer (« ça lui donne l'air bête »), and for strokes slightly thinner, then slightly thicker
again. The values below are the ones validated in the local preview on 2026-11-14. The wordmark was settled last: ten fonts
were compared at real size in the desktop sidebar header, and the maintainer chose Nunito 700, written in lowercase
everywhere (the desktop sidebar still wrote « SOWEL »).

## Goals

1. **One drawing**, defined once, used by the app, the splash, every favicon and PWA icon, and the docs landing.
2. **Two weights of that drawing**: a master (rendered at 32 px and above) and a small one (below 32 px) whose strokes
   are heavier so the face still reads.
3. A **tighter face** that sits where the old one did: eyes closer together, a narrower smile, centred near y = 108 of
   the 160 x 165 viewBox. A first enlarged version, centred near 118, looked low and wide-eyed (« l'air bête »).
4. A **single wordmark**: « sowel » in Nunito 700, lowercase, everywhere a wordmark appears, the desktop sidebar included.
5. A **drift guard**: a test fails when an asset stops carrying the shared drawing.

## Non-Goals

- No change to the palette (`--color-primary` ink, `--color-primary-mid` face), to the house silhouette or to the
  wink animation timing.
- No 3D rendering, no mood faces (day, night) and no showcase site: later steps of spec 209.
- No retaken screenshots: the docs screenshots still show the old sidebar logo, which issue #1127 (retake after
  spec 196) already covers.
- No change to `ui-dist/` (stale checked-in build output, regenerated by the build).

## Functional Requirements

### FR1 — The drawing

The house path is unchanged:
`M100 30 L160 90 Q165 95 160 100 L160 150 Q160 158 152 158 L48 158 Q40 158 40 150 L40 100 Q35 95 40 90 Z`,
viewBox `20 10 160 165`, round caps and joins.

| Part      | Master (>= 32 px)          | Weight | Small (< 32 px)            | Weight |
| --------- | -------------------------- | ------ | -------------------------- | ------ |
| House     | outline path above         | 9      | same path                  | 14.5   |
| Left eye  | `M75 94 Q81 84 87 94`      | 7      | `M74 95 Q81 83 88 95`      | 11.5   |
| Right eye | `M113 94 Q119 84 125 94`   | 7      | `M112 95 Q119 83 126 95`   | 11.5   |
| Smile     | `M72 114 Q100 142 128 114` | 7.5    | `M68 115 Q100 147 132 115` | 12.5   |

The right eye is the one that winks; in `SowelLogo` it is a group translated to the eye centre so the existing
`animateTransform` scale keeps working (master `translate(119, 89)`, path `M-6 5 Q0 -5 6 5`; small path
`M-7 6 Q0 -6 7 6`).

### FR2 — Colours

Unchanged in meaning: the outline is the primary ink, the face the logo mid-blue. In the app these are
`var(--color-primary)` and `var(--color-primary-mid)`; static assets use `#1A4F6E` and `#7FB8D4` (app and PWA) or
`#6FB3D9` (docs landing, as today). PWA icons keep their white tile.

The sidebar (40 px) and the mobile header (24 px) were monochrome and become two-tone like the login: the logo is one
drawing. At 24 px the face (`#7FB8D4`) is about 2:1 against a white page; it is a logotype, not a control, and the
small weight is there to keep it readable. Raised in review: to be confirmed by the maintainer on the running app.

### FR3 — Wordmark

`<text>` « sowel », Nunito 700, `font-size 40`, `letter-spacing -0.4`, centred under the mark in the app (login,
setup, splash; viewBox `20 10 160 205`). The docs landing hero uses Nunito 700 lowercase.
The sidebar wordmark (`Sidebar.tsx`, Nunito 700, `text-title`) reads « sowel » without the letter-spacing it had for the
capitals.

### FR5 — Drift guard

A UI unit test reads `SowelLogo.tsx` and every static asset above and fails when an asset does not carry the shared
house path and the face paths of its weight.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [x] `SowelLogo` renders the master drawing at 32 px and above, the small one below, with the wink on the right eye
- [x] « sowel » (Nunito 700, lowercase) replaces « SOWEL » on the login, setup and splash screens and in the sidebar
- [x] `favicon.svg` (app and docs) and the docs `logo.svg` carry the small weight; `pwa-icon.svg` and `pwa-maskable.svg` the master
- [x] The four PNG icons are regenerated from those sources and read correctly at their size
- [x] The maskable PNG keeps every ink pixel inside the 80 % safe circle (measured)
- [x] `apple-touch-icon` query bumped to `?v=4`
- [x] The docs landing shows the new mark and « sowel » in EN and FR; `mkdocs build --strict` passes
- [x] `design-system/tokens.md` describes the logo weights and the lowercase wordmark
- [x] The drift test passes, and fails when one path, one stroke width or the wordmark of a copy is altered
- [x] `npm run validate` and the docs and spec checks pass

## Edge Cases

- **Dark theme**: the ink is `--color-primary` (light blue in dark), the face `--color-primary-mid` (`#7FB8D4` in both
  themes), so in dark mode the face is a little lighter than the outline. This is how the app already behaves; not
  changed here.
- **Installed PWA**: an existing home-screen icon keeps the old drawing until the user adds it again (iOS reads the
  icon at install). The release notes say so.
- **Sizes between weights**: 24 px (mobile header) uses the small weight, 32 px (wizard) and 40 px (sidebar) the master.
- **Reduced motion**: the wink is an SVG `animateTransform`, as before; unchanged.
