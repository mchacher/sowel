The page a visitor opens in front of the gate

Spec 181 — shared access · the control, to be handled before a line of it is written. The phone is shown in French, the page's default language.

A dark screen, one warm disc, everything else out of the way. What had to be settled is the gesture: a movement, not a button to validate. Four were put side by side, all really playable here, with a mouse or a finger, refusals included. Chosen on 2026-09-23: pull the disc upward. The other three stay under the tabs, so the comparison is still at hand.

One thing from the reference visual that is not reproduced as is

The visual this started from showed a « Ouverture en cours » banner and a house-wide journal: « opened from the application », « closed automatically ». Those are facts about the gate's state. This page is pollable by anyone holding a code, and R4.16 says it never tells whether the gate is open — otherwise a code lent out becomes a listening post on the house. The banner and the journal are kept, but they carry the echo of this phone's own presses: what it asked for, and the travel time running out. The « house-wide journal » switch below shows the other reading, side by side.

On a keyboard: Tab to the disc, then hold Space. A gesture nobody can perform would be a gate nobody can open, so every variant keeps that way out.

The duration shown (8.4 s) is the travel time declared on the equipment; on the gate this was designed against it is 34 s.

What this changes in the spec

R5.20 used to read « the control is Sowel's slide-to-confirm (spec 146) ». It is rewritten around the pull, R5.21 keeps the keyboard's way out through that same slide, and R5.22 states what the dark rendering is allowed to show: this phone's own presses, never the gate's state. The rest of the spec does not move.
